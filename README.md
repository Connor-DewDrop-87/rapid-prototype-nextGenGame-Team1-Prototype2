# Name Of Game: Farming Simulator
**Group Members:** Arijah Urrahman, Connor Almeyda, Diogo Campos, John Yeilding

**Project:** Rapid Prototype #2, Digital Prototyping Development, Mohawk College

## Main Game Mechanic / Feature
Planting plants to gain profit.

## Design Rationale
Our game is a 2D pixelated farm simulator with a focus on survival, resource management, and farming. In order to make money and stay alive, the player assumes the role of a farmer who must manage their resources, raise crops, and care for animals. The primary objective is to develop a straightforward yet captivating gameplay loop in which the user must manage several tasks while strategically allocating their time and resources. We decided on a 2D pixel style since it works well for the farming simulator genre and enables us to make a visually beautiful game while maintaining a realistic scope for our team. Additionally, pixel graphics gives the game a straightforward, identifiable look that can enhance the beauty and coherence of the farm, crops, animals, and surroundings. Since this is a collaborative project, utilizing pixel art enables us to concentrate on developing a unified visual style without complicating the creation of the assets. Growing crops and taking care of animals are the primary gameplay elements. In order to continue creating resources that may be sold for money, players must take care of their crops and make sure their animals are healthy. The farm can then be maintained and improved with the help of the money gained. This results in a never-ending cycle of planting, caring for, gathering, selling, and reinvesting. In addition to this, there is also a timer that adds another level of challenge to the game. Unlike the games where the player can accomplish his tasks without any sense of urgency, this timer makes the player consider the use of his limited amount of time. The player needs to give priority to his crops that need to be attended to, animals that need to be taken care of, and those things that will be beneficial to him. The objective here would be to make the game as simple and as enjoyable as possible, but at the same time, the player should have some real choice when playing the game. We do not want to confuse the player by making too many complex systems for him/her to play; rather, the core mechanisms of the game should be easy enough, but at the same time encourage the player to use his/her time and money in an efficient way. Overall, the design is focused on building an enjoyable and time-sensitive game play environment. The use of pixel graphics helps to maintain an accessible visual design of the game, whereas the agricultural and economic system form the core game mechanics. The presence of the timer brings more excitement into the game, pushing players to plan their actions for survival and success.

## Objective Statement And Player Goal
The prototype's goal is to determine whether a fast-paced, arcade-style farming simulator can offer a captivating and pleasurable gaming experience. The game emphasizes fast decision-making, effective resource management, and time-based challenges as players must finish farming duties, manage crops and animals, and earn money in a set amount of time. 

## Controls
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow Keys or WASD | Movement |
| Spacebar | Planting or uprooting a plant (You can only plant a plant if you have at least 1 of them). Space is also used to buy plants when near the refueler |
| Number Keys (1–6) | Switch which plant is equipped by pressing the number keys |

## How To Play
Move around the farm as your character, plant and harvest crops, feed animals, and gather farm goods. Make money by selling your products and finish as many farming chores as you can before the timer ends.

## Gameplay
Players will have to change between Planterns to plant and uproot the crops. The players can choose to either wait for the crops or go to the Refueller to buy some more plants.

## Level Design
At the moment, there is only one level in the game with the Planterns distributed evenly across the farm for convenience. The Refueller is located at the top right side of the farm, slightly further from the Planterns. Therefore, the player must choose between waiting until their crops mature or traveling to get more plants.
